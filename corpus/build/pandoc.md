# Leek and potato soup

A soup for cold evenings, from the second bed of the allotment.[^1]

| Ingredient | Amount |
|:-----------|-------:|
| Leeks      |      3 |
| Potatoes   |  500 g |
| Stock      |    1 l |

: Ingredients for four bowls

1. Slice the leeks.
2. Dice the potatoes.
3. Simmer for twenty minutes.
